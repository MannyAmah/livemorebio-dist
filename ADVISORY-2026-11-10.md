# Livemore Bio 0.1.0: fidelity advisory (10 November 2026)

**Our rule.** Every part of the twin must work as the real human body does, calculated only from real biology. Nothing it calculates counts as a result until living human tissue or real people confirm it.

**Most of 0.1.0 does not meet that rule yet.** We checked the parts of 0.1.0 that calculate biology against it, and we are withdrawing nearly all of them.

**What this means for you now.**
- 0.1.0 still shows these values. Please do not use them.
- Treat any numbers you have already exported, shared or published from them as withdrawn.
- In 0.1.1, the software will show a reason instead of a number wherever a value is withdrawn.

**Withdrawn: every value 0.1.0 calculates about biology, except the four items below.**

This covers, for example:
- **Glucose and insulin:** the twin's glucose and insulin, meals, foods and lifestyle, and the glucose tolerance test and its "effect" verdicts.
- **Heart values shown by the twin:** every heart-cell value the 0.1.0 twin and console show (APD90, voltage and calcium), the hERG-block results, and the "torsades risk" warning. They are paced at the twin's rule-of-thumb heart rate and include the glucose link and the block setting.
- **Drugs:** drug and metformin effects.
- **Disease and genetics:** disease progression and staging, the diabetes-prevention benchmark, and genotype and stress or sleep effects.
- **Heart rhythm:** heart rate and heart-rate variability.
- **The virtual LIFE Patch.** 0.1.0 has no physical LIFE Patch, so every LIFE Patch reading in 0.1.0 is synthetic, calculated from the withdrawn glucose engine.
- **Generated people:** virtual and synthetic people and their cohorts, including cohorts of "Reference-grounded research persons" (for example the "NHANES older diagnosed diabetes" and "DPP high metabolic risk" strata).
- **The exogenous-insulin program in Cendos** ("paired within-individual arms"). It runs the Hovorka model on a sampled virtual type 1 population, so its glucose responses and its counts of time below 3.9 mmol/L are withdrawn.
- **Other calculations:** the botanical exposure programs, the lobeline protocol, discovery scores, and the "combined" disease score and its ranking.

Many of these are typed-in or rule-of-thumb values, not mechanisms of the body.

**Kept.**
1. **The published O'Hara-Rudy CiPA heart cell,** as a calculation labelled "prediction, untested". It is a paced cell (one beat per second, as in a laboratory preparation) with no drug-block setting. Its equations are integrated correctly. Around them, 0.1.0 has the errors listed below.
2. **The published Hovorka glucose-insulin model of type 1 diabetes** (Hovorka 2004), with its single published parameter set, as a calculation labelled "prediction, untested", after the fixes below. It applies only to type 1 diabetes and is not dosing guidance.
3. **The Human-GEM energy calculation,** labelled as the capacity of a generic human cell network, not a value for you. It is calculated correctly.
4. **Each ChEMBL and Open Targets record,** labelled as that source's own association, after the fixes below.

**Not affected.**
- Measurements you recorded yourself with a real instrument, and files you imported, remain measurements.
- Cohorts made with "Create frozen reference cohort" under "Joint public-source records" are real NHANES public survey records and remain so.
- Updating to 0.1.1 deletes nothing you stored. Stored records that hold withdrawn values will show a reason instead of the numbers.

**Known calculation errors in 0.1.0, fixed or withdrawn in 0.1.1.**

Visible in the app or its local API:
- **Heart-cell APD90.** It can be wrong when a beat lasts nearly as long as the interval between beats (strong potassium-channel block at the twin's highest heart rate), or when a beat fails to recover.
- **The reported heartbeat is not yet settled.** Its APD90 can differ from the settled value by up to 16 ms.
- **Look-ups:**
  - they can report a partly failed look-up as complete and keep it cached;
  - they count records reported only as "greater than" a value as potent (for example ">1000 nM", meaning the half-maximal effect was not reached up to that concentration);
  - the "in genome-scale model (Recon3D)" count always shows 0.
- **The diabetes-prevention benchmark** (local API only). Its result depends on the random seed: 27–42% across seeds, and 36% at the API's default settings, against the trial's 28.9%. Its one scaling value was tuned to that trial figure.
- **Out-of-range inputs.** Some are accepted instead of refused. For example, a negative meal size gives negative glucose. Some inputs make the local Livemore server stop responding while it calculates.
- **The Hovorka model** reports time below 3.9 mmol/L without the body's counter-regulation, and clamps liver glucose output. Both show in the withdrawn exogenous-insulin program.

Only when the engines are called directly from Python:
- **The Hovorka model:**
  - it can miss a short insulin infusion (a few minutes or less) that starts partway through a run;
  - it ignores a bolus given exactly at the run's end;
  - its basal "steady state" is not converged (for example 5.96 instead of 5.05 mmol/L at 0.4 U/h);
  - by default it starts from zero insulin.
- **Full potassium-channel block** in the heart cell gives a wrong APD90.
- **A heart-cell parameter override** can carry over into later runs on the same object.
- **The default glucose engine** can skip a meal in runs longer than a day.
- **The Cendos lab-records module (LIMS)** stores calculated readouts as experiment evidence. 0.1.1 will show a reason instead of readouts calculated by withdrawn engines, and will label any others as calculations, not experiments.

**Nothing produced by 0.1.0 is clinical evidence.** Every value the twin calculates is, at best, a prediction until living human tissue or real people confirm it.

0.1.1 fixes or withdraws each problem above and corrects every label that overstates what an output is.
